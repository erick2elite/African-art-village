Enter the Vision

Inspired by the work of Komera Leadership Center, Ocean Sole, and the Accra Arts District.

“Nothing is wasted when imagination is given the opportunity to create.”

Discarded materials, traditional knowledge, and young imagination are the raw materials of a new economy. We build workshops where scrap becomes sculpture and talent becomes a livelihood.

Curriculum & Production

The Six Pillars

Tap any pillar to view its curriculum and workshop structure.

[ PHOTO PENDING — PILLAR 01 ]
01 +

Fine Arts & Creative Expression

Painters, sculptors, and photographers build portfolios for international exhibitions from Nairobi to Paris.

Students work in shared studios with visiting curators from the Accra Arts District.
[ PHOTO PENDING — PILLAR 02 ]
02 +

Recycling & Environmental Art

Ocean plastics and scrap metal become large-scale public sculptures on the Ocean Sole model.

Ten tonnes of municipal refuse are sorted weekly into raw material for sculpture.
[ PHOTO PENDING — PILLAR 03 ]
03 +

Practical Skills & Fabrication

Carpenters and welders craft architectural furniture from reclaimed eucalyptus and scrap steel.

Every bench, door, and truss is built on site by apprentices.
[ PHOTO PENDING — PILLAR 04 ]
04 +

Digital Creativity & Technology

Students turn physical craft into 3D models, animation, and open-source design blueprints.

Digital files link local workshops to fabricators across Rwanda, Kenya, and Ghana.
[ PHOTO PENDING — PILLAR 05 ]
05 +

Entrepreneurship & Creative Enterprise

Artists learn pricing, copyright registration, and direct export to international collectors.

Graduates retain full copyright and sell directly through our cooperative gallery.
[ PHOTO PENDING — PILLAR 06 ]
06 +

Culture, Heritage & Community Arts

Elders teach Imigongo geometry and natural dye preparation to the next generation.

Workshops preserve Gisaka cow-dung relief techniques alongside contemporary clay firing.
Campus Infrastructure

The Living Ecosystem

The architecture itself will demonstrate sustainability — recycled materials, natural light, rainwater harvesting, solar energy.

[ PHOTO PENDING — ECOSYSTEM ]
01 / ATELIER

Creative Studios

North-facing ateliers for painting, sculpture, and large-format printmaking.

02 / PROTOTYPING

Makerspace

Wood lathes, arc welders, and hand tools running on solar power.

03 / CIRCULARITY

Material Recovery Centre

Sorting bays for municipal plastics, scrap metal, and discarded rubber.

04 / COMPUTATION

Digital Lab

Twelve workstations for 3D modelling, digital drafting, and animation.

05 / EXHIBITION

Gallery

Compressed-earth exhibition hall with natural ventilation and museum-grade daylight.

06 / COMMERCE

Creative Market

Direct-sale stalls for resident artisans to sell to visiting collectors.

07 / MONUMENT

Sculpture Garden

Open-air walking trail featuring monumental works from reclaimed metal.

08 / GATHERING

Amphitheatre

Stepped earth pavilion for poetry, acoustic music, and civic forums.

09 / CULTIVATION

Demonstration Garden

Permaculture beds growing natural indigo, madder, and moringa for dye.

10 / ARCHIVE

Learning Centre

Seminar rooms for business classes, legal workshops, and oral history.
